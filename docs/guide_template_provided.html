    Wilder RSI(14) Momentum: CE > 60.0 / PE < 40.0 to confirm directional trend velocity.

5. Daily Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Execution Schedule
08:45 – 09:10 IST
Pre-Flight Telemetry & Margin Sync

Initialize workstation at http://localhost:8000. Verify WebSocket status displays ONLINE, ensure India VIX is loaded, and check available broker margin.

09:15 – 09:45 IST
30-Minute Opening Range Formation

Engine records opening candle range across NIFTY and BANKNIFTY. No orders are placed. Radar status switches to ORB LOCKED at 09:45 IST once 6 consecutive 5m candles print.

09:45 – 10:00 IST
Morning Retail Shakeout Quarantine

Trading algorithm enters passive observation. All trigger pulses are suppressed to bypass erratic institutional liquidity traps.

10:00 – 13:15 IST
Institutional Scan Window & Automated Execution

Active breakout surveillance window. When a 5-minute candle closes beyond the ORH/ORL range with VWAP and Wilder RSI confluence, bracket entry orders are dispatched to Shoonya with atomic speed.

15:15 IST
Mandatory Intraday Square-Off & EOD Audit

All intraday MIS positions are liquidated at market depth. Final telemetry logs and execution ledger are exported, and trade summaries are dispatched to Telegram.
